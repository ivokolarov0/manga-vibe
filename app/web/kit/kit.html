UI kit

The pieces MangaVibe and its plugins share. How to use, restyle and change them: docs/UI-KIT.md.

Buttons

Fields

Text fields, selects, textareas, checkboxes, and a checkbox with .switch. A .field with x-field: a <label>, a .hint, the control and an .error, tied together for screen readers.

Setting rows

.setting-row with x-field in a .settings-card: a .label (the name in a <span>, a <small> hint) and its control. Dividers go between rows, never under the last one.

Tabs

.tabs with x-tabs: buttons, the active one with .active; data-tab ties a tab to its data-tab-panel; a .count in a tab; .small, .scroll. Left and Right move and pick.

Segmented

.segmented with x-choice: two or three choices side by side, the chosen one .on (a raised tile; .accent: the accent colour). The arrow keys move and pick.

Options

.options with x-choice: one choice per row, like radio buttons, the chosen one .on.

Menu

.menu with x-menu: a button, then a .menu-pop of buttons (an icon, the label, a <small> line, .danger). Try the keyboard: Enter, the arrows, Home, End, a letter, Escape.

Dialog

.modal or .sheet with x-dialog="<close>", a .sheet-head at the top. A bottom sheet on phones, centred on wider screens.

A dialog

A click on the backdrop or Escape closes it, and the focus goes back to the button that opened it. Tab stays inside; the page behind can't be reached or scrolled.

Toast

MangaVibe.toast(text, ms, kind, action): read out by screen readers (an error at once); an action adds a button, and the toast waits while the mouse or the focus is on it.

Progress

.progress with role="progressbar", its value and a name; without aria-valuenow it's busy with no known end.

Badges

.count in a tab or on an icon button; a badge that is only an icon or a number is role="img" with an aria-label, so it's read as words.
